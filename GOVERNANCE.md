# GadgetBuddy Governance

GadgetBuddy Studio AI operates under **Pihlonme Koncern Governance v0.1**.

Canonical governance is maintained in the private `pihlonme/Pihlonme-Os` repository under `GOVERNANCE/`. This file is the public project-facing adoption contract; it does not replace or redefine the canonical Constitution.

## Core values

- **INGENTING ÄR OMÖJLIGT** — investigate before declaring a path impossible; decompose problems, test assumptions and seek alternatives. This never means ignoring evidence, safety, law, privacy, authorization or real constraints.
- **Oändliga möjligheter för utveckling och lärande** — success produces knowledge; failure produces evidence. Preserve useful failures and convert recurring problems into regression tests, checklists or improved architecture.
- **WE ARE ONE** — optimize for the whole system, share learning and surface blockers early. Unity never requires conformity or personal loyalty; evidence-based dissent is protected.

Supporting principles: **Truth over ego**, **Challenge the idea — support the person**, **Unity without conformity**, **Failure → Evidence → Learning → Improvement**, **Ownership without blame**, and **Safety enables ambition**.

## GadgetBuddy enforcement

The existing Test Lab and Challenger remain the technical evidence system. Governance does not replace tests; it governs how results, uncertainty, failures and decisions are handled.

Material changes use the seven-question Values Gate:

1. **Truth** — Is status/evidence accurate and are unknowns visible?
2. **Possibility** — Were reasonable alternatives explored before declaring a path impossible?
3. **One** — Does the change optimize the wider ecosystem rather than silently harm another project/team?
4. **Challenge** — Were material objections considered rather than suppressed?
5. **Learning** — Are reusable lessons/regressions captured where relevant?
6. **Safety** — Are privacy, security, legal, authorization and rollback risks acceptable?
7. **Ownership** — Is the responsible owner and next action explicit?

Valid outcomes are `PASS`, `PASS_WITH_ACTIONS`, and `BLOCKED`. `BLOCKED` means do not merge or release. `PASS_WITH_ACTIONS` must identify the follow-up owner and action and may not waive safety or correctness blockers.

## AI / automation obligations

AI agents and automated workers must never fabricate progress, present planned work as implemented, present unverified work as verified, hide material uncertainty, or broaden permissions merely because a task is blocked. Failures should be isolated, preserved as evidence, fixed at root cause where practical, and retained as regression coverage.

## Authority boundary

Project-local rules may strengthen these requirements but may not weaken canonical governance. Law, platform/security restrictions, safety, privacy, secret-handling rules and explicit user authorization always take precedence.
